# Food information verification procedure

## Current product classification

The business owner confirmed on 2026-08-19 that website products are made
manually after the customer places the order. They are therefore operated as
non-prepacked food, not as food packaged before being offered for sale. Review
this classification whenever products, packaging or production timing changes.

The public site must make allergen information available before purchase. The
site provides a prominent phone contact and shows structured information only
after an administrator has verified it. See the Swedish Food Agency guidance
for [non-prepacked food sold at a distance](https://kontrollwiki.livsmedelsverket.se/artikel/51/distansforsaljning-inte-fardigforpackat).

The owner confirmed on 2026-08-19 that trained staff can provide current
ingredient, allergen and trace information by phone at `072-868 25 92` every
day from 11:00 to 22:00. The menu and checkout must display those hours and
tell customers not to complete an order until they have received the information
they need. If the phone cannot be reliably answered or staff cannot verify the
answer from the current recipe, supplier labels and kitchen routine, pause this
oral flow and publish verified written information before accepting online orders.

## Verification before publication

For every product, the responsible kitchen operator must:

1. reconcile the complete current recipe with every supplier label;
2. identify each regulated allergen in every ingredient;
3. assess shared equipment, storage, handling and other cross-contamination;
4. record applicable trace warnings separately from ingredients that contain an
   allergen;
5. enter the reviewed information in Admin → Lager → Ingredienser;
6. read the final public presentation before accepting orders with that data.

The admin confirmation is an operational attestation, not a substitute for the
underlying recipe, label and kitchen review. Never infer ingredients from
marketing descriptions. Revoke the verification immediately when a recipe,
supplier, label, process or shared-equipment risk changes.

## Individual requests

Because products are prepared after ordering, the kitchen may discuss an
individual adjustment before the order is accepted. Staff must never promise
that a product is allergen-free or that traces are impossible unless a qualified
assessment of the complete kitchen process supports that exact statement.
Record only preparation instructions needed for the order; do not add medical
diagnoses or unnecessary health details to free-text fields.

## Change to prepacked sales

Stop online sale of a product before changing it to prepacked. The existing
structured allergen fields are not the complete mandatory label for prepacked
food. Add and verify every required field, make the information available before
checkout and ensure the delivered package carries the required information.
See the Swedish Food Agency guidance for
[prepacked food sold at a distance](https://kontrollwiki.livsmedelsverket.se/artikel/53/distansforsaljning-fardigforpackade-livsmedel).
